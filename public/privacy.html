Privacy Policy

Food Storage · Last updated November 4, 2026

Food Storage (kunkles.github.io/food-storage) is a free planner for storing emergency food, water and fuel. It runs entirely in your web browser. There is no Food Storage server, no accounts of our own, no ads and no analytics.

What the app stores

Everything you enter — your household, plan, food on hand, water, fuel, prices and settings — is saved in your own browser's local storage on your device. We never receive it. Other people who use the app have their own separate lists.

Sign in with Google (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app asks for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata, which lets it create and read its own hidden app folder in your Google Drive. It uses that folder to save one file, food-storage.json, containing the same list described above, so you can use it on other devices.

  • The app cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files.
  • It reads your Google account's email address only to show you which account you're signed in with.
  • Your data goes directly between your browser and Google. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • The sign-in token is kept in your browser's memory for the session and is not stored on any server.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Food Storage's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising, and no person reads it.

Deleting your data

  • In your browser: clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • In Google Drive: open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Food Storage → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove access: click Sign out in the app, or remove Food Storage at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Third parties

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement). Google sign-in is provided by Google (see Google's privacy policy).

Children

The app is meant for adults planning for their households and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/Kunkles/food-storage/issues.
